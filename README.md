## Team Contributions

### Task 1 – Research
**Contributors:** LM Kabongo, Joshua Peter

LM Kabongo and Joshua Peter worked on the research into workflow management systems and the concepts relevant to TIVIDY. This included workflow definitions and instances, activities, work items, assignment, routing, participants, dependencies, events, auditing, monitoring, reusability and workflow versioning.

### Task 2 – Initial Scope and Scenario
**Contributor:** Kristy Heesen

Kristy Heesen developed the initial scope of TIVIDY and defined the selected University Student Complaint Management scenario. This included distinguishing workflow definitions from running workflow instances and establishing the responsibilities and boundaries of the generic workflow management system.

### Task 3 – UML Activity Diagrams
**Contributors:** Lindokuhle Skosana, Joshua Peter

Lindokuhle Skosana and Joshua Peter worked on the UML Activity Diagrams for the generic TIVIDY workflows and the selected University Student Complaint Management scenario.

### Task 4 – Design Patterns
**Contributors:** Kristy Heesen, Musa Msibi, Lindokuhle Skosana

Kristy Heesen, Musa Msibi and Lindokuhle Skosana worked on identifying and justifying the GoF design patterns used in TIVIDY. This included defining the design problems, pattern participants, collaborations, variation points and how the patterns work together within the workflow management system.
